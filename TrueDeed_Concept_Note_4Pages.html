1. Problem Chosen: P3 — Catch Fraud & Double-Selling

Under Section 5 of the official LandTech 2026 Rule Book, Team TrueDeed tackles Problem P3: Catch Fraud & Double-Selling: "Spot fake documents, the same land sold to more than one buyer, and records that do not match between registration and mutation."

2. Why It Matters in Bangladesh (National Urgency & Evidence)

Land disputes are the single greatest driver of social friction, generational litigation, and judicial congestion in Bangladesh. According to Law Commission of Bangladesh reports and judicial statistics from the Supreme Court, property and boundary disputes account for approximately 70% of the country's ~3.5 million pending civil litigations, immobilizing billions of takas in productive wealth.

The Core Institutional Void: Deed Registration is governed by the Directorate of Registration under the Ministry of Law, whereas Record-of-Rights (Khatian surveys and Mutation) are administered by Upazila AC Land offices under the Ministry of Land. These two jurisdictions operate without real-time algorithmic synchronization during deed execution.

Fraud syndicates exploit this structural disconnect through three disastrous mechanisms:

  • Inheritance Over-Selling (Farayez Violation): Following an ancestor's demise, heirs inherit strictly defined statutory fractional shares under Islamic law and Section 4 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961. Fraudulent heirs routinely conceal female siblings, fabricate unauthorized local heir certificates, and sell 30 to 40 decimals from an estate where their mathematical share is only 15 decimals.
  • Broken Baya Lineage (Fake Preceding Deeds): Authentic title requires an unbroken historical chain of ownership connecting back to state survey records (CS → SA → RS) under Section 17A of the Registration Act 1908. Fraudsters routinely inject fabricated intermediate deeds featuring fictitious sub-registry volume numbers that cannot be verified manually by buyers.
  • The 45-Day Double-Selling Concurrency Window: Under Section 143 of the State Acquisition and Tenancy Act 1950, finalizing an e-Mutation (নামজারি) requires 30 to 45 days post-registration. In this latency window, government databases still reflect the original seller's name, enabling corrupt sellers to execute duplicate sales of the identical plot to multiple buyers across different offices.

3. Proposed Solution: The TrueDeed Infrastructure

TrueDeed is an automated Algorithmic Decision Support System (ADSS) designed to bridge the void between Sub-Registry offices and AC Land offices. Rather than acting as a passive record query portal like e-Porcha, TrueDeed operates as an active pre-execution fraud intercept gate that evaluates title integrity, statutory inheritance quotas, and temporal transaction locks in under 5 seconds before transfer deeds are executed.

6. Main Features of TrueDeed

TrueDeed delivers five targeted innovations resolving Problem P3 at the national administrative level:

  • Farayez Statutory Inheritance Quota Interceptor: Automatically computes lawful fractional entitlement based on Islamic jurisprudence (incorporating Aul proportional abatement, Radd return, and Section 4 of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 for predeceased heirs). Blocks any transaction where claimed selling acreage exceeds legal entitlement.
  • Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) Baya Lineage Tracer: Reconstructs the complete historical chain of title from CS/SA/RS baseline surveys down to the current seller. Flags any fake intermediate deeds, missing mutation orders, or broken seller-buyer continuity.
  • 45-Day Temporal Concurrency Plot Lock: Enforces an active transactional hold on target Daag records across registration desks during the statutory e-mutation window, completely eliminating duplicate sales of the same plot to multiple buyers.
  • Multimodal Bangla Legal OCR & Normalizer: Parses complex, handwritten, and archaic legal Bengali deed scans into normalized machine-readable schemas with side-by-side human-in-the-loop verification screens.
  • Composite TrueDeed Trust Score (0–100%): Provides an explainable, deterministic security metric accompanied by a tamper-evident digital verification certificate with QR-verifiable cryptographic proof.
