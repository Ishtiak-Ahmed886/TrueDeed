1. Problem Chosen: P3 — Catch Fraud & Double-Selling

We have selected Problem P3: Catch Fraud & Double-Selling as defined in Section 5 of the official LandTech 2026 Rule Book: "Spot fake documents, the same land sold to more than one buyer, and records that do not match between registration and mutation."

2. Why It Matters in Bangladesh (National Urgency)

Land disputes are the single greatest driver of social instability, familial violence, and judicial congestion in Bangladesh. Over 70% of the 3.2+ million civil litigations currently pending in national courts originate from fraudulent land titles, trapping generations of citizens in perpetual legal warfare and immobilizing billions of takas in productive capital.

The Core Institutional Flaw: Deed Registration is governed by the Directorate of Registration under the Ministry of Law, whereas Record-of-Rights (Khatian surveys and Mutation) are administered by Upazila AC Land offices under the Ministry of Land. These two jurisdictions operate without real-time synchronization.

Fraud syndicates exploit this structural disconnect through three disastrous mechanisms:

  • Inheritance Over-Selling (Farayez Violation): Following an ancestor's death, heirs inherit strict statutory quotas under the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961. Fraudulent heirs routinely conceal female siblings, fabricate unauthorized local heir certificates, and sell 30 to 40 decimals from an estate where their mathematical share is only 15 decimals.
  • Broken Baya Lineage (Fake Preceding Deeds): Transfer deeds must establish an unbroken chain of ownership connecting back to state survey records (CS → SA → RS) under Section 17A of the Registration Act 1908. Fraudsters inject fabricated intermediate deeds featuring fictitious sub-registry volume books that cannot be verified manually by common buyers.
  • The 45-Day Double-Selling Window: Under Section 143 of the State Acquisition and Tenancy Act 1950, finalizing an e-Mutation (নামজারি) requires 30 to 45 days post-registration. In this latency window, corrupt sellers execute duplicate sales of the identical plot to multiple buyers before the first sale reflects on government servers.

3. Proposed Solution: The TrueDeed Infrastructure

TrueDeed is an automated algorithmic verification layer designed to bridge the void between Sub-Registry offices and AC Land offices. Rather than acting as a passive record query portal like e-Porcha, TrueDeed provides pre-execution fraud intercept algorithms that mathematically evaluate title integrity, statutory inheritance quotas, and temporal transaction locks in under 5 seconds before deeds are executed.

5. Core Technology Stack & Feasibility

TrueDeed is architected for immediate zero-cost execution and maximum deployment feasibility:

  • Backend Framework: Python (FastAPI) delivering asynchronous, sub-second REST microservices.
  • Perception & Vision AI: OpenCV for adaptive binarization, noise filtering, and deskewing, paired with Google Gemini 2.0 Flash Vision API for multimodal Bengali legal entity extraction.
  • Frontend Interface: Next.js 14, React, and Tailwind CSS engineered for an accessible, bilingual (Bangla/English) citizen and bank underwriter dashboard.
  • Data & Concurrency Layer: SQLite for embedded prototyping and PostgreSQL with PostGIS schemas for spatial land coordinates and temporal mutex locks.
  • Cryptographic Security: SHA-256 hashing to generate immutable digital verification audit certificates.
Zero-Cost & Privacy Compliance: In accordance with Section 10 of the LandTech Rule Book, TrueDeed operates entirely on open-source libraries and enterprise free tiers. All testing and demonstrations utilize strictly synthetic dummy records modeled after official Sub-Registry deed templates.

6. Main Features of TrueDeed

Feature 1: Bangla Legal Document OCR & Entity Extraction

Eliminates manual form entry by scanning physical deed documents (handwritten or printed) and directly extracting key structural metadata: District, Upazila, Mouza, JL Number, CS/SA/RS Khatian numbers, Haal and Sabek Daag numbers, directional four boundaries (চৌহদ্দি), and transferred decimal area.

Feature 2: Algorithmic Farayez (Inheritance Math) Validator

Implements statutory mathematical rules of the Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 ($1 \text{ Son} = 2 \text{ Daughters}$). Given the ancestor's baseline land volume and surviving heirs, it calculates each heir's exact mathematical ceiling. If `Transferred_Area > Eligible_Share`, the engine triggers an instant Critical Over-Selling Alert.

Feature 3: Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) for Historical Baya Lineage

Models historical title deeds as a Directed Acyclic Graph connecting original survey records (CS → SA → RS → Mutation → Current Deed). Automatically validates: (1) Temporal Consistency, (2) Party Continuity (Buyer in Deed $N$ matches Seller in Deed $N+1$), and (3) Area Conservation.

Feature 4: 45-Day Temporal Active Plot Lock (Anti-Double Selling)

Enforces a distributed temporal mutex lock on the composite geographic identifier (`District_Upazila_Mouza_Khatian_Daag`) upon transaction initiation. Any secondary transaction attempted on the same plot within the 45-day mutation processing window triggers an immediate Concurrent Transfer Warning.

Feature 5: Objective TrueDeed Trust Score (0–100%)

Aggregates sub-engine validations into a single standardized risk metric: Baya Chain Continuity (35%), Farayez Share Compliance (25%), Active Plot Lock (25%), and Identity/Record Consistency (15%). Active Section 144 injunctions trigger a hard fail-safe override to < 30%.
